What's in the app

Logging that keeps up

Weight, reps, time, distance, whatever the exercise needs. Warmup sets, RPE, RIR, tempo, and per-set notes included.

Rest timer

Auto-starts after each working set. Per-exercise defaults, live countdown on your lock screen, sound or vibration.

Routines and programs

Build your own or start from 7 built-in programs (StrongLifts, 5/3/1, PPL, and more). Supersets, scheduled days, predefined sets.

Progress you can read

PR detection for every exercise type, estimated 1RM, 12+ graphs per exercise, activity heatmap, weekly volume by muscle group.

Your gym, modeled

Track which gym and which machine every set was done on. Machine-scoped PRs, real weight increments, attachments and grips.

Plate calculator

Barbell, machine, and single-lever modes. Finds the best combination from the plates your gym actually has.

Planned vs. actual

Finish a routine workout and the summary shows what you hit against what the plan asked for, set by set.

Bring your history

Import from FitNotes, Strong, Hevy, or StrongLifts. Export every set to CSV or back up the full database anytime.

Deload, handled

Optional deload detection by time, RPE, or stalled lifts. Reduces the load for a week and never lets it drag your next session down.

Backup on your terms

Automatic backup to a private folder in your own Google Drive, or a plain file you keep wherever you like. Optional, off by default.
