A total rep target

Thanks for the idea. These sketches show how a rep target could work in Badger. Nothing is built yet, so your feedback can still change it.

During your workout

Set a target, say 30 pull-ups, and the counter above your sets adds up every set you finish, however you split the reps.

Setting today's target

Open the menu on the exercise and tap Rep target. It starts at your last target, so going from 30 to 31 takes one tap.

If you train from a routine

In a routine, you can plan an exercise by total reps instead of sets. Turn on the rule and Badger adds a rep each time you reach the target.

Two questions for you

  1. Is this counter what you had in mind?
  2. Would you rather set the target yourself before each workout, or plan it in a routine and let Badger add the rep for you?
